## What's new

- You can now optionally **bring your own API keys** to Thread. In Setup, paste an OpenAI or Anthropic key to use their models for Enhance and Ask, or OpenAI for live transcription. Off by default — nothing leaves your Mac until you turn it on
- Keys live in the Mac Keychain on this Mac. Turning a toggle off keeps the key; **Remove from Mac** forgets it here and does not revoke it at OpenAI or Anthropic. Usage is billed to your own provider account
