Thread is a private meeting companion for macOS. Start it before a call and it listens, transcribes live, and helps you turn that into notes, summaries, and follow-ups — without anything leaving your Mac.

There is no account, no sign-up, and no cloud. Sessions are plain Markdown files in folders you choose.

## What's in 1.0

- **Live transcription** of your microphone and everyone else on the call, on-device
- **Rich notes** beside the transcript, with the usual keyboard shortcuts
- **Enhance** rewrites rough notes into a structured summary — overview, key points, decisions, and action items — automatically when you stop, or whenever you ask
- **Ask** chats over this session or your whole library
- **Notch** (optional) — a Liquid Glass strip on the MacBook notch to start or stop recording, see the timer, and take notes while a call is going
- **Named speakers** (experimental, off by default) — transcript lines can show participant names from Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams
- **Tasks** pulled from the meeting, as a checklist you can tick off
- **Smart term correction** for names and jargon Thread has learned from you
- **Search** across every note, transcript, and title
- **Multiple recordings per session**, each timestamped
- **Apple Notes** (optional) — copy a saved session into Notes for reading on your phone. Thread still keeps the Markdown file

Thread needs **macOS 26 (Tahoe)** and an Apple Intelligence–capable Mac for Enhance and Ask. Transcription and note-taking work without Apple Intelligence.
