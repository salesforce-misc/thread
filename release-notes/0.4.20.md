## What's new

- Speaker names now work beyond Google Meet: Microsoft Teams on the web and in the desktop app, and Zoom on the web, all attribute transcript lines to the person talking
- Zoom's desktop app publishes no speaking indicator, so Thread works it out from who is unmuted — reliable on a two-person call, and on larger calls when one person is talking
- Your own lines are no longer occasionally swallowed when you and the other side talk over each other
- **Ask anything** sits under the note itself now, so you can ask questions while a call is still recording. It's a one-off conversation — only your notes and the transcript are saved to the file
- The library actions moved to the foot of the sidebar, where the list fades out behind them
- Setup drops the mute-sync and speaker-identification sections, which no longer needed to be there
