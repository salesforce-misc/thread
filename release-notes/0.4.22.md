## What's new

- A new **Notch** overlay hugs your MacBook's notch with a Liquid Glass panel: start or stop recording from the left, and open a note-scoped chat on the right. Turn it on in Setup
- Recording from the notch asks for consent right there, and the chat answers only from this meeting's transcript and notes — with a Liquid Glass input that stays readable in light and dark
- **Speaker names** is now an opt-in, experimental setting. It's off by default; turning it on explains the Accessibility permission it needs before asking for it
- Setup's on/off switches now show a clearer gray when off, so they're easy to read over the glass cards in light mode
